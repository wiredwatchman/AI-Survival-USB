AI SURVIVAL USB: QUICK CARD (print this and keep it with the drive)

START
  1. Plug in the USB drive.
  2. Double-click start.bat. Wait about 30 seconds.
  3. When it says READY, type your question and press Enter.
  4. Type /bye to leave the chat.

STOP
  1. Double-click stop.bat.
  2. Wait for the "safe to eject" message.
  3. Eject the drive from File Explorer.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
  - "ollama.exe is not on this drive": copy ollama.exe and the lib folder
    from %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Ollama. See START-HERE.txt.
  - "The model folder is missing": copy everything from
    %USERPROFILE%\.ollama\models into ollama\models on the drive.
  - Very slow: copy the whole USB folder to C:\AI-Survival and run start.bat there.
  - Not enough memory: close other programs.
  - Antivirus warning: Ollama only listens on this computer (port 11434).

ALWAYS CHECK IMPORTANT ANSWERS against a printed or official source.
The AI can be wrong.
In an emergency, call 911.
